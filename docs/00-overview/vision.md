# Vision

**Product vision (proposed):** provide a trustworthy platform for managing financial accounts and, over time, the services that safely move money, maintain a ledger, notify users, and report activity.

Target users are banking-product teams, client applications, support/operations staff, and future platform services. The present release supports account-management learning and integration; it does not implement customer onboarding, transactions, payments, authentication, or a ledger.

Professional approach: grow around clear business ownership and auditable data. Learning explanation: each service owns one understandable job rather than putting all banking logic in one application. The trade-off is operational overhead; microservices should not be split merely for appearance.
